# Change log

Plugin versioning is derived from Moodle releases, it does not comply with the semantic versioning standard.

The format of this change log follows the advice given at [Keep a CHANGELOG](https://keepachangelog.com).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Czech translation and AI-produced previews of German, French, Italian, Portuguese and Spanish translations
- team cohorts are described and cohort usage in frameworks and teams is listed in advanced cohort management

### Changed

- supervisors, subordinates and team members are selected in a users picker panel with identity columns and paging instead of an autocomplete
- migration to new forms library
- cohort names link to cohort management, the advanced cohort management page is used when installed
- reports have an always visible filter bar, the standard filters form is available as Advanced filtering

### Fixed

- remove action was missing or failing with an error in the list of all team members of a framework
